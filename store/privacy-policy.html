Privacy Policy — Vector: Cloud Prompt Security

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Vector is a browser extension that analyses cloud infrastructure prompts for missing security controls.

What we collect

Nothing. The extension has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no server of its own.

Where your data goes

  • Rule-based analysis runs entirely in your browser. Your prompt is never sent anywhere.
  • Optional "Deep scan":
    • If the browser's built-in on-device model is available, analysis runs locally on your device. Nothing is transmitted.
    • If you configure a third-party API key, and only when you click Deep scan, the prompt text is sent to the endpoint you configured, under that provider's terms and privacy policy.

What is stored locally

Settings (optional API key, base URL, model, policy JSON) and a short history of your last analyses are stored with chrome.storage.local on your device. This never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the extension.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • storage — save your settings and local history.
  • clipboardWrite — copy the improved prompt when you click Copy.
  • Host access to AI chat sites — read the prompt in the chat box only after you click the Vector button.
  • https://api.openai.com/* — used only if you configure an API key for the optional deep scan.

Children's privacy

This is a developer tool and is not directed at children.

Contact
